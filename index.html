About

I am an undergraduate researcher at UC Berkeley working on evaluation and post-training for LLM agents. My research focuses on automated graders, adaptive failure taxonomies, gold-free candidate ranking from execution traces, and prompt and program optimization. I currently lead FailureRank and have worked on AdaMAST, ProofGrader and ProofBench, and learned error diagnosis for GEPA. My research has been advised by Ion Stoica, Sewon Min, and Matei Zaharia.
