About

I am a computer science undergraduate at UC Berkeley working with the Berkeley AI Research (BAIR) and Sky Computing labs. I work with Ion Stoica and have also collaborated with Sewon Min and Matei Zaharia.

My research focuses on machine learning and AI systems, particularly evaluation, optimization, and learning from structured feedback. I am also interested in simulation and world models.

Current research

My work on FailureRank and GEPA extensions investigates evaluation and optimization from execution evidence without reference answers. I am exploring both alternative reward evaluators and structured feedback for improvement in reinforcement learning, evolutionary algorithms, and continual learning.

I am also developing taxonomies and judges tailored to specific objectives, including correctness, robustness, and required behaviors. I am studying how these differ from taxonomies designed for corrective feedback.
